# ReviewPort Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 13, 2026

ReviewPort for TikTok Shop ("ReviewPort", "the extension", "we", "us") is an independent browser extension that lets a user filter and export reviews from TikTok Shop product pages. This policy explains what information the extension handles and how that information is used.

## Summary

ReviewPort processes review information locally in the user’s Chrome browser. It does not operate a remote server, does not require an account, does not use analytics or advertising software, and does not send review content to an AI, translation, tracking, or other third-party service.

## Information handled by the extension

When a user explicitly starts a scan on a TikTok Shop product page, ReviewPort reads the review information made available on that page. Depending on the product page, this can include the reviewer’s displayed username, star rating, review text, selected product SKU, review date, and URLs of review photos.

The extension also stores the user’s local settings, including selected star range, page limit, export format, and product handle.

## How information is used

ReviewPort uses review information only to provide the user-requested functions: filtering review records, previewing saved records in its local Full Review Preview tab, and producing a CSV download. Exported usernames preserve the exact name displayed on TikTok Shop, including any masking already shown by TikTok Shop. The Full Review Preview reads the same locally stored review data; it does not re-scan TikTok Shop, trigger pagination, or transmit review data. The extension does not publish reviews, post content, make purchases, modify product pages, or contact reviewers.

## Storage and retention

Review data and preferences are stored locally in Chrome extension storage on the user’s device. The user can clear saved review data at any time with the extension’s **Clear saved results** control. ReviewPort also removes saved review data older than seven days when Chrome runs its scheduled local cleanup.

## Data sharing and sale

ReviewPort does not transmit review content or local settings to any remote server. It does not sell, rent, share, or use review information for advertising, profiling, or analytics. CSV files are downloaded only to the user’s browser; the user controls any subsequent use, storage, editing, sharing, or import of those files.

## Permissions

ReviewPort requests only the permissions needed for its single purpose. The `activeTab` and TikTok Shop host permission allow the extension to access the current TikTok Shop product page when the user starts a scan. The `scripting` permission helps connect to that active page. The `storage` permission keeps scan state and preferences locally. The `alarms` permission supports the seven-day local cleanup.

## Security

Because ReviewPort does not send review content to a server, review data is not transmitted by the extension. Users should still protect downloaded CSV files and use them in accordance with applicable laws, platform terms, and the privacy rights of reviewers.

## Third-party services and affiliations

ReviewPort is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok, TikTok Shop, Judge.me, Google, or Shopify. TikTok Shop and Judge.me are referenced only to describe compatible pages and export formats.
